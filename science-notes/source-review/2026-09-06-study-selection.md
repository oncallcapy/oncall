# ONCALL Science Notes — study-selection record

**Initial source review:** 2026-09-06

**Access recheck:** 2026-09-07

**Status:** reviewable editorial source set; named human scientific review not yet recorded

## What this file is

The opening Science Notes collection contains five editorial dossiers and exactly three publications in each dossier. The set was built to show how different designs answer different questions: prevention is not the same endpoint as cardiovascular events; a procedure trial is not a methods study; a care pathway is not a communication technique; mechanism is not clinical efficacy; and a claims field is not the whole patient.

This is a deliberately bounded educational selection. **It is not a systematic review, clinical guideline, treatment recommendation, investment analysis, or proof that a paired company produced, sponsored, or is validated by a study.** Pair symbols and company names are navigation metadata only. Publication titles remain unchanged for bibliographic accuracy.

## Selection and verification rules

- Each dossier has three independent study identities. Corrections are attached to their original articles and are not counted as additional studies.
- Public claims are limited to the designs, findings, and limitations recorded in the source packages and rechecked against an authoritative publication, index, or evidence-summary page.
- No exact effect size is transferred where a full source or correction could not be reopened.
- Abstract-only, publisher-summary, and full-text access are labelled separately.
- A named human reviewer must check the complete public wording, sponsor and conflict disclosures, endpoint definitions, uncertainty, corrections, and current clinical context before any live publication.

## The fifteen-study ledger

### Metabolic medicine and cardiometabolic outcomes

1. **Knowler et al., 2002 — diabetes prevention.** Multicenter three-group randomized trial in adults at high risk of diabetes. The 2026-09-06 review used the original article copy for methods and results; [PubMed PMID 11832527](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11832527/) is the persistent index record and DOI is [10.1056/NEJMoa012512](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa012512). On 2026-09-07, the standard PubMed page opened without readable body text. The supported intensive program should not be assumed to transfer unchanged to routine care, and this report does not establish lifelong or cardiovascular-mortality benefit.
2. **Look AHEAD Research Group, 2013 — risk factors versus events.** Randomized trial comparing intensive lifestyle intervention with support and education in type 2 diabetes. [PubMed PMID 23796131](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23796131/) and DOI [10.1056/NEJMoa1212914](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1212914) were verified on 2026-09-06; the standard PubMed page had no readable body text on 2026-09-07. A [publisher correction](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMx140022), DOI `10.1056/NEJMx140022`, concerns a secondary composite label in Table 2. The correction page returned HTTP 403 on recheck; the dossier therefore does not transfer corrected numerical values.
3. **Lincoff et al., 2023 — direct cardiovascular outcomes.** Double-blind placebo-controlled event-driven trial in adults with established cardiovascular disease, overweight or obesity, and no diabetes history. The [PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37952131/) and DOI [10.1056/NEJMoa2307563](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2307563) were readable on 2026-09-07. It reports the clinical-event result and higher permanent discontinuation for adverse events. The study was industry funded; the sponsor's role, protocol, analysis, and disclosures require full-paper human review before publication-level interpretation.

### Clinical evidence, procedures, and appraisal

4. **Al-Lamee et al., 2018 — ORBITA.** Double-blind sham-controlled randomized procedure trial after medical-therapy optimization. The [PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29103656/) and DOI [10.1016/S0140-6736(17)32714-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(17)32714-9) were accessible on 2026-09-07. PubMed also links an [erratum record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29323656/), DOI `10.1016/S0140-6736(17)33366-4`, PMID `29323656`; the record triggered an access challenge and the full erratum text was not accessible. Its contents are not inferred, and no possibly affected detail is transferred from the erratum. The study's small sample, short follow-up, and narrow setting do not settle mortality, every procedure indication, or the later evidence base.
5. **Zampieri et al., 2024 — BEST-Living.** Systematic review and Bayesian individual participant data meta-analysis of six randomized trials in critical care. The [PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38043564/) and DOI [10.1016/S2213-2600(23)00417-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/S2213-2600(23)00417-4) were accessible on 2026-09-07. Risk of bias in included trials and a distinct traumatic-brain-injury pattern prevent a one-size-fits-all fluid claim. “Living” in the title does not establish updating through 2026.
6. **Moustgaard et al., 2020 — blinding meta-epidemiology.** Observational comparison of effect estimates across 1,153 trials within 142 meta-analyses. The [PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31964641/) and DOI [10.1136/bmj.l6802](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.l6802) were accessible on 2026-09-07. A [BMJ correction](https://www.bmj.com/content/368/bmj.m358), DOI `10.1136/bmj.m358`, corrects author information; its page returned HTTP 403 and its PubMed record triggered an access challenge on recheck. Residual confounding means the study does not show that blinding is unnecessary.

### Digital care and patient communication

7. **McManus et al., 2021 — HOME BP.** Randomized evaluation of self-monitoring, digital feedback, and guided management as a care pathway. [PubMed PMID 33468518](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33468518/) and DOI [10.1136/bmj.m4858](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.m4858) were verified on 2026-09-06; the standard PubMed page had no readable body text on 2026-09-07. Internet access was required, and the pathway result cannot be assigned to an app alone or converted into a cardiovascular-event claim.
8. **Chair et al., 2024 — telephone consultation.** Systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized trials examining safety, service use, satisfaction, and workload. The [PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37624630/) and DOI [10.1089/tmj.2023.0002](https://doi.org/10.1089/tmj.2023.0002) were accessible on 2026-09-07. Service settings and comparators were heterogeneous; outcome-level risk-of-bias and certainty tables still require full-text review.
9. **Talevski et al., 2020 — teach-back.** Systematic review of implementation and effects across varied designs. The [publisher full text](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0231350), DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0231350`, was accessible on 2026-09-07. Heterogeneity prevented meta-analysis, and favorable-study counts should not be treated as a pooled effect or validation of a platform.

### mRNA evidence, mechanism, and safety

10. **Graña et al., 2022 — vaccine evidence synthesis.** Cochrane systematic review of randomized trials with GRADE assessment. The [Cochrane evidence summary](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD015477_what-are-benefits-and-risks-vaccines-preventing-covid-19), DOI `10.1002/14651858.CD015477`, was accessible on 2026-09-07. Its search ended on 2021-11-05; it is not a current-variant estimate or a 2026 vaccination schedule.
11. **Turner et al., 2021 — germinal-center response.** Longitudinal mechanistic study using blood and fine-needle lymph-node aspirates. The [Nature article page](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03738-2), DOI `10.1038/s41586-021-03738-2`, was accessible on 2026-09-07; no claim is made that every subscription-only component was reviewed. Lymph-node sampling involved 14 participants, and cellular persistence is not lifelong clinical protection.
12. **Barda et al., 2021 — nationwide safety analysis.** Matched observational cohorts assessing selected adverse events in a 42-day window, with a separate infection comparison. The [PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34432976/) and DOI [10.1056/NEJMoa2110475](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2110475) were accessible on 2026-09-07. Randomization was absent; separate cohorts are not a personalized direct net-benefit calculation, and age, sex, long-term, current-variant, and current-regimen transfer remain limited.

### Health systems and recorded outcomes

13. **Baicker et al., 2013 — Oregon insurance experiment.** Randomized natural experiment based on lottery selection for the opportunity to apply for coverage. The [PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23635051/) and DOI [10.1056/NEJMsa1212321](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMsa1212321) were accessible on 2026-09-07. Lottery selection did not guarantee enrollment; the two-year, state-specific results are multidimensional and cannot be compressed into “insurance works” or “insurance does not work.”
14. **Obermeyer et al., 2019 — proxy bias in population algorithms.** Retrospective empirical evaluation of a deployed risk algorithm. The [PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31649194/) and DOI [10.1126/science.aax2342](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aax2342) were accessible on 2026-09-07. The identified cost-versus-need mechanism belongs to a specific algorithm and system; it is not an effect estimate for every model or a clinical treatment trial.
15. **Song et al., 2010 — regional diagnostic practice.** Observational natural experiment using Medicare claims before and after regional moves. The [PubMed Central full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2924574/) and DOI [10.1056/NEJMsa0910881](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMsa0910881) were accessible on 2026-09-07. A correction exists at DOI [10.1056/NEJMx100034](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMx100034), but the correction page could not be reopened. The dossier therefore reproduces no disputed table values or percentages.

## Release gate

The JSON records intentionally omit `reviewer` and `reviewedAt`. Passing schema and integrity tests means the files are structurally reviewable; it does not certify medical accuracy, establish clinical validation, or authorize public deployment. A named human reviewer must approve the final rendered copy and record a review date before the live Science collection can be presented as reviewed.
