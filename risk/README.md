## Contracts and dependencies

[PAR documentation](https://par.family/docs) states that its contracts are unaudited (source record accessed 2026-09-06). Documentation, source availability and a successful transaction do not establish absence of vulnerabilities. Contract behaviour, permissions and upgrade dependencies require direct review against the actual deployment. Bugs, compromised dependencies or incorrect assumptions could cause loss.

Quote-asset contracts, pricing or oracle inputs, RPC services, APIs and frontends can fail, change or return stale or incomplete information. A timestamped ACTIVE API entry is a metadata observation. It is not proof of current priceability, a reliable oracle or a safe route. Check chain identity, deployed code, contract addresses, source versions and observation times before relying on any later launch evidence.

## Value and quote-asset dependence

ONCALL and planned quote assets may be volatile and lose value. Five markets do not guarantee diversification, returns, tracking or safety. Different quote assets introduce their own price, issuer, product-structure and availability risks; apparent prices across books may diverge.

Stock Tokens provide economic exposure and do not grant legal or beneficial rights in the issuer of the underlying security. Robinhood describes them as debt securities issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited. A pool involving a Stock Token does not collateralize ONCALL with the underlying security or grant its holders shares. The [official product terms and explanation](https://robinhood.com/rhj/stocktokens/) govern product-specific rights and restrictions.
